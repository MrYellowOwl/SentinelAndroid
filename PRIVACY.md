# Sentinel Security — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 19, 2026_

Sentinel Security ("the app") is a security checkup tool that runs entirely on your device.

**What the app collects:** nothing. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or server of any kind.

**What stays on your device**
- Network scan results (device addresses, names and open services on the Wi-Fi network you choose to scan).
- Website audit results (the address you enter and the security findings for it).

These results are stored privately inside the app on your phone. They are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. You can dismiss individual findings or clear all of them at any time, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

**Network activity:** The app only contacts devices on your current private network when you start a network scan, and only contacts a website when you run an audit on it. Those connections go directly from your phone to that device or website.

**Notifications and watches:** If you turn on alerts, scan summaries appear as notifications on your phone. Your paired smartwatch may mirror them. The optional Wear OS app receives a summary (issue counts, last scan time, scan progress) from your phone over a direct, encrypted Bluetooth/Wi-Fi connection managed by Google's Wear OS services. None of this goes to us.

**Sharing:** A report leaves your device only when you tap "Share report" and choose where to send it.

**Permissions:** Internet (to run scans and audits), network state (to detect your current Wi-Fi network) and, only if you turn alerts on, notifications. No location, contacts, storage, camera or microphone access is requested.

**Children:** The app is not directed at children under 13.

**Responsible use:** Only scan networks and websites you own or are authorized to test.
