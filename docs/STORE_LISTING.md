# Google Play listing — Sentinel Security

**App name (30 max):** Sentinel Security

**Short description (80 max):**
Scan your Wi-Fi for risky devices and check your website's HTTPS and headers.

**Full description:**
Sentinel is a private security checkup for networks and websites you own.

NETWORK SCAN
• Finds devices on your home or office Wi-Fi
• Labels your router and phone, shows device names where available
• Flags risky open services like Telnet, VNC, exposed Docker, Remote Desktop, file sharing and databases
• Explains each risk in plain language with a clear fix

WEBSITE AUDIT
• Checks that HTTP redirects to HTTPS
• Verifies the TLS certificate: expired, untrusted, wrong hostname or expiring soon
• Reviews security headers: HSTS, Content Security Policy, clickjacking protection, nosniff, Referrer-Policy
• Spots insecure cookies and server version disclosure

FINDINGS & REPORTS
• Issues sorted by severity, with filters
• Re-scanning updates results, so fixed issues disappear
• Share an HTML report with your team or client

ALERTS & WATCH
• Get notified when a scan finishes or finds high-severity issues, with a one-tap "Scan again"
• Alerts show up on your paired smartwatch
• Wear OS app: see your issue counts and start a scan from your wrist

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Results stay on your phone unless you share a report.

Sentinel performs safe, non-destructive checks only. Use it only on networks and websites you own or have permission to test.
